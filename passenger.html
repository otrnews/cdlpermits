CDL Passenger Vehicles practice test

For the P endorsement, needed to drive buses with 16 or more people including the driver. The real test has 20 questions in most states.

Want to learn it first? Take the free lesson

Study all 18 questions with answers
  1. How much allowed hazardous material may a bus carry in total?
    No more than 500 pounds
    Buses may carry limited amounts of allowed hazmat: no more than 500 pounds total and no more than 100 pounds of any one class.
  2. Which of these is never allowed on a bus?
    Division 2.3 poison gas
    Division 2.3 poison gas, liquid Class 6 poisons, tear gas and irritating materials are forbidden on buses.
  3. What is the standee line?
    A line on the floor that passengers may not stand in front of
    No rider may stand forward of the standee line while the bus is moving, because they would block the driver's view.
  4. At a railroad crossing, a bus must stop how far from the nearest rail?
    Between 15 and 50 feet
    Stop 15 to 50 feet from the tracks, open the forward door if it helps you see or hear, then cross without shifting gears.
  5. A bus must stop at least how far before a drawbridge without a signal light or attendant?
    50 feet
    Stop at least 50 feet before the draw and look to make sure it is completely closed before crossing.
  6. Where should you discharge a disruptive passenger?
    At the next safe, well-lit stop
    Put them off at a safe, lighted place, like the next scheduled stop. Don't leave them in an unsafe spot.
  7. Can you refuel a bus with passengers on board?
    Not in a closed building with riders aboard
    Never refuel in a closed building with passengers on board, and avoid refueling with riders aboard whenever you can.
  8. Before driving, emergency exits on a bus should be:
    Checked and closed, with the handles working
    Emergency exits must be closed while driving but able to open, and never blocked.
  9. What should you never use in place of the parking brake on a transit bus?
    The brake-door interlock
    The interlock holds the bus while the rear door is open, but it releases when the door closes. Always set the parking brake.
  10. Where should baggage and freight be stored on a bus?
    Secured so the driver and passengers can move freely and exits aren't blocked
    Aisles and exits must stay clear, and loose items can fly forward in a sudden stop.
  11. During a bus pre-trip, what should you check inside?
    Handholds, railings, floor covering, signal devices and emergency exits
    Check that handrails, floors, signal devices and emergency exits are in good shape and working.
  12. Small arms ammunition labeled ORM-D on a bus is:
    Allowed
    Buses may carry small-arms ammunition labeled ORM-D and certain other limited items.
  13. Which of these may a bus carry?
    Emergency hospital supplies and drugs
    Emergency hospital supplies and drugs are allowed on buses. The others are forbidden.
  14. When may a bus cross railroad tracks without stopping?
    When a police officer or flagger directs traffic
    Buses must stop at crossings unless a police officer or flagger directs traffic, a signal shows green, or the crossing is marked exempt.
  15. Where do many bus crashes happen?
    At intersections
    Use caution at intersections, even with a green light, since many bus crashes happen there.
  16. Why do buses often crash on curves?
    Too much speed, especially in rain or snow
    Buses can skid or roll over on curves taken too fast. The posted speed may be too fast for a bus.
  17. Should you chat with passengers while driving?
    No, avoid distracting conversations while driving
    Talking with riders takes your attention off the road.
  18. Which of these should you watch for riders bringing on board?
    Car batteries or gasoline
    Watch for common hazardous items like car batteries and gasoline, which riders may not realize are dangerous.
