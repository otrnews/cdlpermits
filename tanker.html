CDL Tank Vehicles practice test

For the N endorsement, needed to haul liquids or gases in bulk. The real test has 20 questions in most states.

Study all 10 questions with answers
  1. When is a tank endorsement required?
    When hauling liquid or gas in a tank of 119 gallons or more, or tanks totaling 1,000 gallons or more
    The N endorsement is required for individual tanks of 119+ gallons or a total of 1,000+ gallons that are permanently or temporarily attached.
  2. What is liquid surge?
    Movement of liquid in a partly filled tank that pushes the truck in the direction the liquid moves
    Surge can push a stopped truck forward into an intersection or make it hard to control on slippery roads.
  3. What do baffles inside a tank control?
    Front-to-back surge, but not side-to-side surge
    Baffles slow front-to-back liquid movement. Side-to-side surge still happens and can cause a rollover.
  4. Why are smooth-bore tanks especially hard to drive?
    They have no baffles, so front-to-back surge is very strong
    Smooth-bore tanks, often used for food products like milk, let liquid surge freely. Brake gently and allow extra distance.
  5. What is outage?
    Room left in a tank for the liquid to expand
    Liquids expand as they warm up. Never fill a tank completely; leave outage for expansion.
  6. Why are bulkheads used in tanks?
    To divide the tank into compartments
    Bulkheads split the tank into separate compartments. Watch weight distribution when loading and unloading them.
  7. Why do tankers have a high risk of rollover?
    They have a high center of gravity and the load can shift
    Most of the weight sits high off the road, and moving liquid can shift that weight suddenly in curves.
  8. How should you handle curves in a tank vehicle?
    Slow down before the curve, then accelerate slightly through it
    Braking in a curve adds to surge and rollover risk. Slow down before you enter and keep steady power through.
  9. When coming to a stop in a tanker, you should:
    Keep steady brake pressure and avoid releasing the brakes too soon
    Releasing too early lets surge push the truck forward. Brake smoothly and early, with steady pressure.
  10. Which load is generally the hardest to control because of surge?
    A partly filled tank
    A partial load has room for the liquid to move. That moving weight is what makes surge dangerous.
